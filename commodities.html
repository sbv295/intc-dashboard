Prices are computed as (COMEX futures price ÷ 31.1034768g per troy oz) × 10 × the live USD/INR rate — a close proxy for bullion-grade (≈99.5%+ fine) metal, not an official jeweller/dealer quote. Real-world 24K gold and 999 silver rates in India run higher once import duty, GST and making charges are added. 52-week range uses today's live USD/INR rate applied across the year, so it's approximate for older dates.
